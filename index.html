Formulation Chemistry

R&D Lab & Clinical Excellence

Our 17-year R&D laboratory integrates gas chromatography mass spectrometry (GC-MS) with bio-fermentation tech to engineer barrier-compatible active skincare.

01

100,000-Class Aseptic Facility

Controlled humidity and positive pressure air-filtration systems guarantee zero microbial contamination during filling.

02

GC-MS Purity Profiling

Every batch of raw botanical oil and fermented extract is fingerprinted for molecular purity and potency before formulation.

03

Bio-compatible Lipid Mimicry

Formulas feature identical lipid ratios (ceramides:cholesterol:fatty acids) designed to integrate seamlessly into human stratum corneum.

04

3-Stage Accelerated Stability

Thermal shock (45°C to -10°C), UV degradation, and centrifuge tests ensure a guaranteed 24-month unopened shelf life.
